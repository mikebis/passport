Privacy

passport is a local-first travel map. You can use it without an account. Usage analytics, when enabled by the site operator, is separate from your travel data.

Your travel data

Your places, statuses and favourites are saved in this browser. Theme, projection and desktop sidebar preferences are also stored locally. JSON imports and exports are processed on your device; keep downloaded backups safe. Clearing site storage removes the browser's saved map and preferences.

Share-picture names and generated images stay on your device, with names held only in page memory. The app does not upload pictures. A picture's QR opens the app, not a published copy of your personal map.

Optional Google Drive backup

Google sign-in and Drive requests begin only when you choose to connect. The app requests identity and private app-data access, not access to your other Drive files. Tokens stay in page memory. Drive snapshots are private, not end-to-end encrypted, and old versions are retained.

Signing out disconnects backup but keeps your map on this device. Remove saved app data or revoke access through your Google account settings separately. The app retains account association and pending backup metadata locally to reconcile future connections safely.

Optional Google Analytics

If the operator configures Google Analytics 4, the public production app automatically collects basic usage information without a consent prompt: page views, sessions, estimated users, device information and approximate visitor countries. This describes where visitors connect from, not where they have travelled.

Google receives browser and network information and may use first-party analytics cookies and pseudonymous browser identifiers to distinguish visits. User counts are estimates, not exact unique people. The app does not send your travel statuses, favourites, names, Google sign-in identity, exported data or picture contents to analytics. It supplies a fixed page title, the app's base address without query strings or fragments, and at most the referring site's origin. Advertising signals are disabled; operators must keep automatic interaction measurement and advertising features disabled.

Analytics is skipped in development, on recognized local/private addresses, and when your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control. Enable one of those signals before loading the app to opt out. You can also block analytics using browser controls. Clearing this site's cookies removes its existing analytics identifiers; it does not prevent new cookies on later eligible visits or delete previously collected data at Google.

This privacy page loads no analytics. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from partner sites. Hosting providers may separately process ordinary request logs.

For site operators

Analytics is disabled unless a measurement ID is configured. Enabling it without prior consent may not meet legal requirements in your jurisdiction. A privacy notice or browser-signal opt-out does not replace legally required prior consent. Resolve applicable consent, disclosure and retention requirements before enabling analytics. Property settings control retention and must match your actual policy.
